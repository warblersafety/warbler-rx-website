    Your demo & privacy

    Use fictional patient details. Your edited mock record is sent to ElevenLabs to personalize the demo. For phone calls, Twilio connects the AI call and may send a verification text when verification is enabled. Your number is used for this requested demo, not marketing.

    For an in-browser conversation, your browser asks for microphone permission. End that conversation to stop microphone access. ElevenLabs processes the conversation audio for both demo modes. Audio recording storage is disabled; conversation text is retained for up to one day.

    A first-party security cookie helps enforce demo limits. A separate quota database stores hashed browser, network and phone-recipient identifiers with start times, rather than raw phone numbers or mock records. Quota records may remain beyond the 24-hour usage window. People on the same network share its three-start allowance. Vercel retains ordinary website request logs.

    Questions? noah@warbler-health.com

    A fictional conversation

    This website demo is an AI addiction-clinic role-play, not medical care. Try a missed visit, ride problem or concern about returning. Use fictional details. It cannot arrange refills, appointments or clinical follow-up, and it cannot contact a clinician. The phone option places the single demo call you request to a number you control. It may first send a verification text.

    Sessions last up to two minutes. A rolling 24-hour window allows three starts per visitor and network, ten starts across the website, and one phone callback per recipient number. A reserved start counts even if the connection fails. You can still explore the example workflows after reaching a limit. Discuss medication decisions with your pharmacist or prescribing clinician.

    How this demo is protected

    Provider credentials stay on the server. Single-use voice-session links connect your browser to the configured agent. Shared usage counters reserve each start before a connection is allowed. If the counter cannot be checked, new calls pause until it is available.

    Conversation summaries require a signed, expiring receipt. The website returns reviewed summary labels rather than transcripts. Audio recording storage is disabled, and conversation text is retained for up to one day.

    This public demo is for fictional information. Production addiction treatment use requires separate agreements and readiness review; the demo does not establish HIPAA compliance.
