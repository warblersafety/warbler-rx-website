    About these numbers

    These are program-level medians, with different eligible episode populations at each follow-up period; the connecting line is a visual guide. Episodes began July 2022–June 2023. Fifteen days without services counted as discontinuation. Transfers may count as nonretention. The figures do not measure treatment completion or Warbler’s effectiveness.

    What Warbler won’t do.

    Patients in early recovery have heard enough judgment. Warbler is designed to sound like the most patient person on your staff — and to know when to hand off.

    Give medical advice
    Dosing and medication questions belong with a nurse or prescriber.
    Shame or lecture
    Person-first language, without blame or guilt about missed visits.
    Act without consent
    A demo care request requires the patient’s agreement to staff follow-up.
    Handle a crisis alone
    Urgent concerns need immediate human help. This demo cannot arrange emergency care.

    Your microphone & privacy

    When you start the demo, your browser asks for microphone permission. A third party AI voice-to-text service processes your audio live. Audio recording storage is disabled; conversation text is retained for up to one day. Please use fictional details.

    End the conversation to stop microphone access. A first-party security cookie and hashed browser and network identifiers enforce demo limits. A separate quota database stores only those hashes and start times, with up to ten recent starts; expired entries are removed when the next request arrives. People on the same network share its three-start allowance. Vercel retains ordinary website request logs.

    Questions? noah@warbler-health.com

    A fictional conversation

    This website demo is an AI addiction-clinic role-play, not medical care. Try a missed visit, ride problem or concern about returning. Use fictional details. It cannot arrange refills, appointments or clinical follow-up, and it does not send texts or contact a clinician.

    Sessions last up to two minutes. A rolling 24-hour window allows three starts per visitor and network, and ten starts across the website. A reserved start counts even if the connection fails. You can still explore the example workflows after reaching a limit. Discuss medication decisions with your pharmacist or prescribing clinician.

    How this demo is protected

    Provider credentials stay on the server. Single-use voice-session links connect your browser to the configured agent. Shared usage counters reserve each start before a connection is allowed. If the counter cannot be checked, new calls pause until it is available.

    Conversation summaries require a signed, expiring receipt. The website returns reviewed summary labels rather than transcripts. Audio recording storage is disabled, and conversation text is retained for up to one day.

    This public demo is for fictional information. Production addiction treatment use requires separate agreements and readiness review; the demo does not establish HIPAA compliance.
