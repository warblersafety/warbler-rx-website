A conversation that helps your team act.

Warbler uses the treatment context your clinic shares and relevant details from previous calls.

Illustrative excerpt with a fictional patient. AI disclosure and identity verification completed before this exchange.

Warbler: We missed you this morning, Daniel. What got in the way?

Daniel: My car broke down. I couldn’t get a ride.

Warbler: Would help with a ride make it possible to come in today?

Daniel: Yes, this afternoon.

Warbler: I’ll let the team know you need help with transportation and would like to come in this afternoon.

Your team receives:

Daniel R., day 12 of treatment. Missed this morning’s visit after his car broke down. Requests transportation help to return this afternoon. Staff follow-up needed.

Patient privacy. Clinic oversight.

HIPAA and 42 CFR Part 2

We provide a business associate agreement and contractual commitments addressing Part 2. Your patient data never trains models for other clients.

Discreet outreach

Neutral caller ID. Voicemails omit your clinic’s name and the reason for calling. Treatment details are discussed only after identity verification.

Patient choice

Calls disclose that the assistant is AI, offer a human alternative, and honor opt-outs.

Your team approves the approach

We prepare scripts, consent language, and escalation procedures for your team’s review before launch.
