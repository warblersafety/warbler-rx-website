VOICE DEMO

Your microphone & privacy

When you start the demo, your browser asks for microphone permission. ElevenLabs processes your audio live. Audio recording storage is disabled; conversation text is retained for up to one day. Please use fictional details.

End the conversation to stop microphone access. The demo uses a hashed connection identifier to limit repeated sessions. Vercel retains ordinary website request logs.

Questions? hello@warblersafety.com

DEMO USE

A fictional conversation

This website demo is an AI role-play, not medical care. Use fictional details. It cannot arrange refills, appointments or clinical follow-up, and it does not send texts or contact a clinician.

Sessions last up to two minutes and are subject to availability and usage limits. Discuss medication decisions with your pharmacist or prescribing clinician.

WEBSITE DEMO

How this demo is protected

Provider credentials stay on the server. Temporary voice-session tokens connect your browser to the configured agent. Usage checks run before a connection is allowed.

Conversation summaries require a signed, expiring receipt. The website returns reviewed summary labels rather than transcripts. Audio recording storage is disabled, and conversation text is retained for up to one day.

This public demo is for fictional information. Production pharmacy use requires separate agreements and readiness review; the demo does not establish HIPAA compliance.
