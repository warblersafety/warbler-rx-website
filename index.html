About these numbers

Fictional conversation

Warbler: Hi, I'm Warbler, an AI assistant. Is now a good time for a quick check-in?

Patient: Yes. I've missed appointments since my work schedule changed.

Warbler: Thanks for telling me. What's making it hard to get to your visits?

Patient: My shifts start earlier now. I want to keep coming, but I need a different appointment time.

Warbler: Would you like your clinic's scheduling team to call you about options?

Patient: Yes, please. A call would help.

Warbler: I'll note that you'd like a callback to discuss a different time. Your clinic would confirm any change.

Illustrative only. No appointment or callback is arranged.

Your microphone & privacy

When you start the demo, your browser asks for microphone permission. A third party AI voice-to-text service processes your audio live. Audio recording storage is disabled; conversation text is retained for up to one day. Please use fictional details.

End the conversation to stop microphone access. The demo uses a hashed connection identifier to limit repeated sessions. Vercel retains ordinary website request logs.

A fictional conversation

This website demo is an AI addiction-clinic role-play, not medical care. Try a missed visit, ride problem or concern about returning. Use fictional details. It cannot arrange refills, appointments or clinical follow-up, and it does not send texts or contact a clinician.

Sessions last up to two minutes and are subject to availability and usage limits. Discuss medication decisions with your pharmacist or prescribing clinician.

How this demo is protected

Provider credentials stay on the server. Temporary voice-session tokens connect your browser to the configured agent. Usage checks run before a connection is allowed.

Conversation summaries require a signed, expiring receipt. The website returns reviewed summary labels rather than transcripts. Audio recording storage is disabled, and conversation text is retained for up to one day.

This public demo is for fictional information. Production addiction treatment use requires separate agreements and readiness review; the demo does not establish HIPAA compliance.
